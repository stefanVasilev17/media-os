## Policy and safety
Medium human-created material for wider distribution: https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473-Medium-s-Distribution-Guidelines-How-curators-review-stories-for-Boost-General-and-Network-Distribution and https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/22576852947223-Artificial-Intelligence-AI-content-policy. AI-generated draft requires substantive human authorship and truthful disclosure before approval for the chosen zero-follower route. No unchanged original generated seed can pass that gate. Actual authorship is Director attestation, not mechanically proven. DEV AI policy https://dev.to/guidelines-for-ai-assisted-articles-on-dev/ prohibits promotional/brand-first AI articles; seed is standalone educational only, not a traffic funnel. InfoQ human author support only: https://www.infoq.com/guidelines/. DZone, HN, Reddit and newsletters conservative research-only in V1; current exact community/publication eligibility must be checked by Director. No paid API or automatic posting, no account connection. Platform registry includes independent/partial follower dependency, discovery mechanism, authoring guidance and policy URLs. Review requires author, policy and rights attestations, review evidence (record current policy date/destination), current complete master and Publishing editorial/rights QA. No automatic verification of external publication or author identity beyond existing authenticated Director identity. Manual publication record requires URL and date; external URL not fetched by service. The UI flags the mandatory Medium article and its readiness; it does not change Phase 1's separate release gate. Third-party editorial acceptance never blocks video launch. No guaranteed organic reach.
