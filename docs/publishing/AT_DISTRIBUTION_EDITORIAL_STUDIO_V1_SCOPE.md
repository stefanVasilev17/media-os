# Architectural Thinking — Distribution & Editorial Studio V1
2026-11-27. Director-requested scope addition before implementation. Requirements confirmed by Director: a separate episode tab for complete articles/posts/storytelling and legitimate technical community discussion/distribution; one Medium article for EVERY long-form episode. Detailed UX and implementation choices below are proposed, not implemented or independently Director-LOCKED. No external publication authorized.

## Product goal
One completed long-form episode -> full publishing package, direct-extract Reels (AT-PUB-0002 unchanged), one complete Medium article, tailored educational distribution assets. Expand relevant organic reach and professional authority, not spam, raw post volume, manufactured engagement or guaranteed RPM. All eligible assets are written in English for the engineering audience, with author review. Draft complete content rather than placeholders/outlines, except where platform policies require human authorship or where evidence/URLs are pending. A ready draft is not published or a guarantee of placement.

## Proposed tab and workflow
Add one Distribution tab to the existing episode page; no separate app/service. Show article library, platform-specific posts, discussion opportunities, campaign calendar, published links, analytics/learning. Use existing Java backend, PostgreSQL, React and authenticated storage. Reuse episode script, current approved master, source references, chapter/semantic anchors, existing diagrams/frames, thumbnail, and approved publishing metadata. Draft creation can use the existing subscription-first assisted workflow; no new paid AI/provider, external account connection or new infrastructure without separate permission. Drafts are versioned, editable, copyable/exportable (Markdown/plain text), and reviewed by Director before publication. Store actual destination link and date only when verified or Director supplied. No autonomous posting in V1.

## Content types and default package per episode (proposed)
- Medium: REQUIRED one complete standalone technical article per episode, approximately 1200–2000 words as a guide, not quota. Title/subtitle, original opening/story, architecture mechanics, factual evidence and conceptual boundary, engineering lessons and trade-offs, captions/alt text for existing diagrams, sources, one natural link to video when available, disclosures. Human author substantively reviews/edits and stands behind article. No invented experience. Verify actual primary/final report and any pending factual corrections before approval. Publish free-access by default to support discovery, not paywall AI-generated writing. Label generated/assisted text/images per current rules. No false claim of human-only writing.
- LinkedIn: 2–3 distinct complete drafts per episode (story, technical insight, trade-off discussion), use existing image/frame or eligible direct-extract clip, relevant link where natural.
- DEV/Hashnode: optional substantive engineering article/adaptation, not a promotional funnel or backlink campaign. Check each site's policy, author knowledge, disclosures, and canonical source URL for permitted reposts. Do not prepare generated comments on DEV.
- Reddit: optional community-specific relevant discussion opportunity, with exact community rules checked immediately before post. Where generated text is permitted and the post is genuine educational contribution, prepare complete transparent draft; otherwise human-only notes or skip. No unsolicited messages, spam, vote requests or mass duplicate posts.
- Hacker News: human-only participation; track relevant original source/eligible submission and research reference links, no generated or AI-edited submission text/comments or automated posting; no vote solicitation. Do not treat as recurring promotion channel.
- X/Bluesky: optional concise educational insight/thread and approved link, adapted to platform.
- Engineering publications/newsletters (e.g. InfoQ, The New Stack, DZone, relevant specialist communities): optional pitches/submission opportunities based on actual editorial policy and topical fit. Never claim placement or acceptance; human-only when required. No fabricated first-hand incident participation.
- Existing direct-extract Reels: per-clip title, caption, context, hashtags, eligible link, no extra visual/voice production. Respect AT-PUB-0002.
- Community interactions: track real discussion links, questions and notes, with human-written replies where required; never fabricate discussions, votes, or users. No auto-commenting.

## Proposed data and acceptance
Episode-linked campaign and versioned distribution items: platform, destination/community, artifact type, title, full body, asset references, source/version lineage, target date, policy check timestamp/source, AI disclosure/author review requirement, source-of-truth links, status, published URL/date, optional real metrics and conclusions. Statuses DRAFT / REVIEW / APPROVED / READY_TO_PUBLISH / PUBLISHED / SKIPPED / BLOCKED; policy and unresolved factual/rights checks fail closed. No placeholder links marked ready. Reusable templates and previous-episode lessons; no generic duplicate drafts. V1 supports draft/review/edit/copy/export, manual calendar and actual URL logging; no separate paid model, bot, crawler, social API integration or new service. Later analytics integrations are separate scope. Measure real traffic and referrals where available; avoid invented attribution. End-to-end test: EP004 one real full Medium draft, tailored complete eligible posts, real source links and original asset references; Director review and no automatic publication. Exact posting cadence, number of optional assets, UI layout and platform mix proposed, not locked. Existing factual, audio, SRT, logo-rights and asset archival gates remain unchanged.
