2026-11-27. Director-requested scope addition before implementation. Requirements confirmed by Director: a separate episode tab for complete articles/posts/storytelling and legitimate technical community discussion/distribution; one Medium article for EVERY long-form episode. Detailed UX and implementation choices below are proposed, not implemented or independently Director-LOCKED. No external publication authorized.

## Product goal
One long-form episode -> full publishing package, direct-extract Reels (AT-PUB-0002 unchanged), a mandatory Medium article plus as many distinct qualifying articles as possible, and tailored educational distribution assets ready for the launch window. Expand relevant organic reach and professional authority, not spam, raw post volume, manufactured engagement or guaranteed RPM. All eligible assets are written in English for the engineering audience, with author review. Draft complete content rather than placeholders/outlines, except where platform policies require human authorship or where evidence/URLs are pending. A ready draft is not published or a guarantee of placement.

## Director requirement — Launch-First editorial distribution (AT-DIST-0001, 2026-11-27)
The Director explicitly wants as many high-quality technical articles as possible from the outset, to support reach from the moment the main YouTube video launches. This is not a fixed article count or an instruction to mass-post duplicates. One complete Medium article remains a mandatory minimum for EVERY episode; actively identify and prepare every additional genuinely distinct, technically substantive, platform-eligible article/angle the episode supports, with no arbitrary upper cap. Begin editorial planning from the locked script/research, alongside production; finalize drafts against approved master, current source evidence and rights checks. Do not wait until after launch to start writing. Prepare full texts, titles/subtitles, relevant existing visuals/captions/alt text, sources, disclosures, platform-specific versions and a coordinated launch calendar before public release; mark actual pending video links as pending and bind the real public URL at launch. No invented URLs or claims. Draft-ready, author-approved, platform-eligible, scheduled and actually published are distinct statuses.
Prioritize a launch wave at or shortly after the main video's release (proposed first 24–48 hours), then stagger remaining distinct material over the following days according to relevance, platform rules and audience experience. An optional pre-launch teaser must be approved and must not imply the video is already public. No forced simultaneous posting to every community, repetitive SEO pages, promotional spam, automated commenting or artificial engagement. Third-party editorial acceptance/publication dates cannot be guaranteed and are not a mandatory prerequisite for releasing the video. Director reviews/approves each eligible publication; V1 manual posting only. Existing AI/human-authorship policies and AT-PUB-0001/0002 remain binding. Reach is an objective, not guaranteed by volume.
Proposed planning target, not a mandatory quota: 3–5 distinct substantive technical articles where supported, plus platform-tailored posts, discussion opportunities and direct-extract Reels. More is encouraged if each contribution has unique engineering value and eligible destination. Track article/topic overlap, canonical URLs for permitted republication, actual publication date/link, referral traffic and subsequent lessons. No new visual development, paid API, new service or automatic publishing implied by this requirement.

## Proposed tab and workflow
Add one Distribution tab to the existing episode page; no separate app/service. Show article library, platform-specific posts, discussion opportunities, campaign calendar, published links, analytics/learning. Use existing Java backend, PostgreSQL, React and authenticated storage. Reuse episode script, current approved master, source references, chapter/semantic anchors, existing diagrams/frames, thumbnail, and approved publishing metadata. Draft creation can use the existing subscription-first assisted workflow; no new paid AI/provider, external account connection or new infrastructure without separate permission. Drafts are versioned, editable, copyable/exportable (Markdown/plain text), and reviewed by Director before publication. Store actual destination link and date only when verified or Director supplied. No autonomous posting in V1.

## Content types and default package per episode (proposed)
- Medium: REQUIRED one complete standalone technical article per episode, approximately 1200–2000 words as a guide, not quota. Title/subtitle, original opening/story, architecture mechanics, factual evidence and conceptual boundary, engineering lessons and trade-offs, captions/alt text for existing diagrams, sources, one natural link to video when available, disclosures. Human author substantively reviews/edits and stands behind article. No invented experience. Verify actual primary/final report and any pending factual corrections before approval. Publish free-access by default to support discovery, not paywall AI-generated writing. Label generated/assisted text/images per current rules. No false claim of human-only writing.
- LinkedIn: 2–3 distinct complete drafts per episode (story, technical insight, trade-off discussion), use existing image/frame or eligible direct-extract clip, relevant link where natural.
- DEV/Hashnode: optional substantive engineering article/adaptation, not a promotional funnel or backlink campaign. Check each site's policy, author knowledge, disclosures, and canonical source URL for permitted reposts. Do not prepare generated comments on DEV.
- Reddit: optional community-specific relevant discussion opportunity, with exact community rules checked immediately before post. Where generated text is permitted and the post is genuine educational contribution, prepare complete transparent draft; otherwise human-only notes or skip. No unsolicited messages, spam, vote requests or mass duplicate posts.
- Hacker News: human-only participation; track relevant original source/eligible submission and research reference links, no generated or AI-edited submission text/comments or automated posting; no vote solicitation. Do not treat as recurring promotion channel.
- X/Bluesky: optional concise educational insight/thread and approved link, adapted to platform.
- Engineering publications/newsletters (e.g. InfoQ, DZone, relevant specialist communities; The New Stack paused for guest bylines): optional pitches/submission opportunities based on actual editorial policy and topical fit. Never claim placement or acceptance; human-only when required. No fabricated first-hand incident participation.
- Existing direct-extract Reels: per-clip title, caption, context, hashtags, eligible link, no extra visual/voice production. Respect AT-PUB-0002.
- Community interactions: track real discussion links, questions and notes, with human-written replies where required; never fabricate discussions, votes, or users. No auto-commenting.
