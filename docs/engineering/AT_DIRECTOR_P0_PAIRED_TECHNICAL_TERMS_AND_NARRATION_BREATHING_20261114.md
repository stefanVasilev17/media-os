## Permanent Director corrections — four production and editorial rules

1. **Hold a technical-term definition across its full explanation.** If the narrator returns to the term later in the same teaching beat, the container must not disappear just before the second meaningful explanation or comparison. Readability duration is determined by the actual aligned spoken concept interval, not a fixed arbitrary nine-second animation cap. Hold until the relevant teaching beat finishes; fade gently, do not flash or rotate generic labels.
2. **The first real spoken technical term must have a visible teaching aid.** For eligible glossary terms with a meaningful spoken definition (already required), align the very first card to the exact first verified word-level ElevenLabs occurrence. If the narrator repeats that term later in a comparison, show it again at the second mention rather than assuming viewers remember the earlier overlay. If the first mention has too little room for readable dwell, omit instead of flash; do not invent timing.
3. **Related terms are allowed together, never overlapping.** The earlier default “one glossary callout at a time” has an intentional exception: when two concepts are directly contrasted or taught together, keep their **TERM + definition cards simultaneously visible**, in a clear aligned vertical stack with real spacing, readable type and no overlap or obstruction of the Live Architecture World. This is not permission to bring back repetitive general captions. For EP003 SHOT02 the Director explicitly wants **LATENCY ABOVE THROUGHPUT**, including the second time the narrator says Throughput.
4. **Start moving first; let the narration breathe.** A new SHOT must begin on the **exact preceding Director-LOCKED last encoded visual frame**, then advance the same living map, camera and request particles continuously with no perceived edit. For EP003 SHOT02 specifically, the Director requires **exactly 2 seconds / 60 frames at 30 FPS of living animation before the first sound of the locked SHOT02 voice**. This is a 2-second silent prefix at final mux, NEVER a new TTS take or editing/stretching of approved speech. Shift **ALL** voice-driven HEAR→SEE cue frames by the same +60, extend composition by 60 frames, and test audio/video sync and encoded first-frame identity. Future SHOT boundaries default to ~2 seconds narrator-free breathing unless the Director sets another shot-specific value; always use real selected audio and P0 continuity.
