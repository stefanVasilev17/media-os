# EP001 Creator Memory Event — Narration Simplicity Correction

Date: 2026-10-02

Type: CREATOR_CORRECTION

## Creator correction

The EP001 narration must target a senior audience through the quality of reasoning, not through dense technical vocabulary. The script should explain what the system is doing in simple, natural English so that the architectural idea is easy to understand even when the underlying concept is advanced.

## Locked narration rules introduced by this correction

- Prefer plain-English descriptions of responsibilities and cause/effect over technical terminology in spoken narration.
- Technical component names may remain visible as labels in the Living Architecture Map, but narration should use them only when naming the component materially improves understanding.
- When a technical term is necessary, explain the role in plain language first or immediately around the term; do not stack several technical terms in one sentence.
- Senior-level depth must come from reasoning about trust, responsibility, dependencies, failure, state, and consequences — not from jargon density.
- Avoid technology-name dumping and avoid narration that sounds like documentation.
- Prefer natural spoken English over compact technical phrasing such as `client`, `backend`, `request assembly`, `payload`, `headers/context`, `eligible instance`, or similar terms when a simpler phrase communicates the same idea.
- On-screen labels can carry precise system names while the voice explains what they mean in human language.
- Repetition is controlled: do not repeat the same word, phrase, or rhetorical construction more than three times in sequence. Prefer no more than two repetitions when the third adds no real value.
- Avoid rhythmic list-like constructions such as `one X, one Y, one Z` unless they create a clear storytelling payoff. Clarity is more important than rhetorical symmetry.
- AHA lines may remain short and punchy, but the surrounding explanation must stay simple and conversational.

## Impact on Script Pass 1

The previously drafted Script Pass 1 v0.1 is NOT approved and must be rewritten before locking. Its story intent, visual logic, AHA 01 concept, and chapter boundaries remain valid, but the narration must be simplified according to the rules above.

## Precedence

This correction overrides any earlier wording guidance that could be interpreted as encouraging technical vocabulary merely because the target audience includes senior engineers.
