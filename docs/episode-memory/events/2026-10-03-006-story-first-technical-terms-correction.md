# EP001 Creator Correction — Story-first technical terminology

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: LOCKED creator correction
Scope: EP001 and global Architectural Thinking script standard

Technical terms must not be presented as a sequence of definitions or as if the viewer is being tested. Narration should feel like a story. Terms from the active Spline object should appear naturally inside causal sentences, with their meaning inferred from what is happening in the story.

Do not use glossary-style patterns such as: "X means...", "Y is different...", "Then there is Z..." repeatedly.

Preferred pattern: describe the situation and consequence first, then let the exact diagram term appear naturally inside the same sentence or thought. The viewer should understand the term almost accidentally because the story makes its function obvious.

The script may still mention all important primary and secondary Spline objects, but they must be woven into the narrative rather than enumerated.

This correction supersedes the glossary-like version of SHOT 10 drafted immediately before it.
