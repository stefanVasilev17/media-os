# Creator Correction — Do Not Say AHA Labels in Narration

Date: 2026-10-03
Episode: EP001 — What Really Happens When You Click Login?
Status: CREATOR CORRECTION / ACTIVE RULE

## Decision

Narration must never say meta-production labels such as "this is the first AHA moment", "this is the second AHA moment", "AHA moment", "Reel moment", or any equivalent wording that exposes the production structure to the viewer.

AHA moments remain internal production/story labels only. The script should deliver the insight directly as a natural sentence inside the story.

## Applied correction

SHOT 09 must use the direct insight:

> The Auth Service is healthy, but Login is still broken.

without introducing it as an AHA moment.

## Scope

This rule applies to EP001 and all future Architectural Thinking scripts unless explicitly overridden by the creator.
